> **Pools are in development.** There's nothing to install yet, and nothing here works today.

Pools won't need an installation of their own: they're being built into Puddle, so a Pool will be something you create
or join from the Puddle you already run. This page will cover that once it works:

- creating a Pool, and inviting people;
- joining one from an invite;
- choosing whether your Puddle **seeds** (keeps the Pool's content and serves it to other members), and how much
  history it keeps;
- Auth Puddles and roles.

Until then, [install Puddle]({{ '/wiki/puddle/installation/' | relative_url }}), and follow Pools on
[ezp2p/puddle#52](https://github.com/ezp2p/puddle/issues/52) and the
[Pools project board](https://github.com/orgs/ezp2p/projects/2).

The [Pools dev build](https://github.com/ezp2p/puddle/releases/tag/dev-pools-build) is Puddle built from the
`dev-pools` branch, replaced on every change to it. It's for the people building Pools, and may not work at all.
