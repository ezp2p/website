[Pools]({{ '/pools/' | relative_url }}) are in development, so there's nothing to set up yet. This is where the guide
to creating, joining and seeding a Pool will go once they're built.

Until then:

- The design, and what has been decided so far, is
  [ezp2p/puddle#52](https://github.com/ezp2p/puddle/issues/52).
- The work is tracked on the [Pools project board](https://github.com/orgs/ezp2p/projects/2).
- Pools are built on the `dev-pools` branch of Puddle. If you want to try them early, the
  [Pools dev build](https://github.com/ezp2p/puddle/releases/tag/dev-pools-build) is replaced on every change to it,
  and may not work at all.
