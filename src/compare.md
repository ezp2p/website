# Chat that doesn't need anyone's servers

<p class="lede">Discord, WhatsApp and Signal all run through one company's servers. Puddle doesn't run through anyone's.</p>

Every message you send on Discord, WhatsApp or Signal goes to that company's servers first, then on to the person
you're talking to. If those servers go down, so does your chat. If the company changes its rules, closes your account or
closes altogether, there's nothing you can do about it.

**Puddle takes the servers out.** Your Puddle talks straight to your friends' Puddles, peer to peer. Your chats live on
your own computer, and there's no company in the middle at all.

<div class="compare-scroll compare-glance" markdown="1">

| | **Puddle** | Discord | WhatsApp | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Every message goes through the company's servers** | **No.** Straight from you to them | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Stops working when the company's servers are down** | **No.** There are none | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Needs the internet** | **No.** Works on a home network or VPN too | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **To sign up you need** | **Nothing.** Your identity is your own keys | An email or phone number | A phone number | A phone number |
| **Someone else can ban you or shut it down** | **No** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **End-to-end encrypted** | **Yes** | No, only calls | Yes | Yes |
| **Communities with channels and voice rooms** | **Yes,** with Ponds | Yes | Communities, no voice rooms | No |

</div>

<div class="strengths">
  <div class="strength">
    <h3>Straight from you to them</h3>
    <p>No server in the middle to store, scan or lose your messages. They go from your Puddle to your friend's, and
    nowhere else.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="strength">
    <h3>No internet required</h3>
    <p>Puddle only talks to the addresses you give it. A family can run it on their home network and a VPN, and never
    expose it to the internet at all.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="strength">
    <h3>No sign-up. Ever.</h3>
    <p>No email, no phone number, no account with anyone. Your identity is made on your own machine, and only you hold
    the phrase that recovers it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="strength">
    <h3>Yours to keep</h3>
    <p>Nobody can switch it off, ban you or change the deal. Your Puddle keeps running for as long as you run it.</p>
  </div>
</div>

**Want the community side too?** A [Pond]({{ '/pond/' | relative_url }}) gives you Discord-style channels, roles and
voice rooms, on a server you or a friend runs, joined from the same Puddle.
